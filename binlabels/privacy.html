Privacy Policy

Bin Labels · last updated 6 September 2026

Bin Labels collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server. The app has no network code at all. Your photos and labels never leave your iPhone.

What the app stores, and where

  • The photos you take or choose. A copy is kept inside the app's own storage so your labels survive a restart. It is never uploaded.
  • The names you type and each label's text size and crop.
  • Your page settings — paper size, labels per page, whether cut marks print.

All of it lives in the app's private container on the device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no backup on our side, because there is no our side.

What the app never does

  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No account creation, so there is no account to delete.
  • No access to your photo library beyond the photos you explicitly pick.

Permissions

  • Camera — only to photograph the item that goes in the bin.
  • Photo Library — only to let you choose a photo you already took.

Either one alone is enough to use the app. If you decline both, the app still opens and explains what it needs; it does not block or crash.

Printing

Printing uses Apple's own print system (AirPrint). The document is created on your device and handed to iOS, which sends it to the printer you pick. It does not pass through us.

Children

The app is made for teachers, not for children, and it collects no data from anyone. It contains no advertising, no external links, and no user-to-user communication.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date. Since the app collects nothing, a change would most likely mean a feature was added that needs explaining — and that explanation would appear here before the feature ships.
