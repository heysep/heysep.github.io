Bin Labels

Bin Labels turns a photo and a name into a printable label, and lays several of them onto one page for an ordinary printer. It works entirely offline.

Making a label

  • Tap Add, then take a photo or pick one from your library.
  • Drag the photo to move it in the square frame; pinch to zoom. That sets the crop.
  • Type the name and choose a text size: S, M, L or XL.
  • Tap Save. The label appears on the page.

Changing several labels at once

Press and hold any label to enter selection mode, then tap the others. The bar at the bottom deletes them together or changes all their text sizes at once.

Printing

  • Tap Print to choose paper (US Letter or A4), how many labels per page (2, 4, 6 or 8), and whether cut marks print.
  • Every page is sent at once, not one at a time.
  • Print at 100%. If your printer dialog offers "Scale to fit" or "Fit to page", turn it off — otherwise the labels come out slightly smaller than the preview.
  • Save PDF exports the same document if you would rather print it later or from a computer.

Things people ask

  • Can I use my own label sheets? Not yet. The app prints onto plain paper with cut marks, so you cut the labels out.
  • Why can't I type emoji in a name? The printing typeface has no emoji, so they would silently vanish on paper. The editor warns you before that happens.
  • Do my photos go anywhere? No. The app has no network access. See the privacy policy.
  • Is there a subscription? No. One purchase, everything included, no ads.
