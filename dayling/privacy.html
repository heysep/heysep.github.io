Privacy Policy

Dayling: Countdown Pet Widget · Last updated September 2, 2026

Your dates stay on your phone. There is no account, no login and no server of ours. If you use iCloud, a copy is kept in your own iCloud account so you can restore it on a new iPhone. Dayling counts days until or since a date and shows a pixel pet that grows toward it on your Home Screen widget.

What we do not collect

  • Name, email, phone number, date of birth or any other identity data
  • Location, contacts, calendar, call or message history
  • Photos, other than one you deliberately pick as a card background (it is copied into the app's own storage and never uploaded)
  • Usage analytics or crash reporters (none are included)

What stays on your device

DataWhere

Deleting the app deletes everything on the phone. The iCloud copy stays in your account until you turn off iCloud for Dayling or delete it from iCloud settings. We hold no copy and cannot restore it.

Advertising

Dayling is free and supported by Google AdMob ads (banner, full-screen and optional rewarded video). To serve ads, the AdMob SDK sends Google device information (model, OS version, language), the app name, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data.

After the first screen, iOS asks once whether to allow tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you allow it, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for personalized ads; if you don't, you get non-personalized ads. Every feature works the same either way. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google's advertising data policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Purchases

Removing ads and egg packs are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Purchases can be restored from Settings inside the app.

Children

Dayling is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. Any change that collects more data will be announced in the app update notes.
