Dayling Support

Countdown & days-since widgets with a pixel pet that grows toward your date.

Adding the widget

  1. Open Dayling and add a date. It takes widget slot 1 automatically.
  2. Long-press your Home Screen, tap the + button, search for Dayling.
  3. Pick the small or medium size. "Countdown 1" shows slot 1, "Countdown 2" shows slot 2.

The widget stopped updating

The widget carries 30 days of frames. Open the app once in a while and it tops them up. If it says "Open the app to refresh", do that.

Purchases

Bought "Remove ads" on another device or reinstalled? Settings → Restore purchases.
