Dopamine Warrior

The less you look at your phone, the more the otter grows.

A screen-time app built only from interventions that have actually been tested. Not a blocker — a moment of friction. When you reach for an app you said you wanted less of, Ollie asks you to take one breath first. Most of the time, that is enough.

Ollie is on your side. He is not there to make you feel bad about your phone; he is the one guarding the door. Every time you walk away, he collects a pebble.

Everything happens on your device. Ollie literally cannot see which apps you picked — only how many.
