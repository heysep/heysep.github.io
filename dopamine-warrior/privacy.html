Privacy Policy

Dopamine Warrior · effective 10 August 2026

Dopamine Warrior helps you use your phone less. It does that entirely on your device. This page describes every piece of data the app touches and the single case in which anything leaves your phone.

1. Your screen-time data never leaves your device

When you choose which apps to shield, iOS shows you Apple's own app picker and hands Dopamine Warrior back a set of opaque tokens. Those tokens carry no app name, no bundle identifier, and no icon. The app cannot see which apps you picked — only how many.

Those tokens, and the state of your shield, are stored in a private app group on your device, shared only with Dopamine Warrior's own three extensions. They are never transmitted anywhere. Apple's Screen Time framework is designed so that this data cannot be exfiltrated, and this app has no server, no backend, and no analytics of its own to send it to.

2. Purchases

Payment is processed by Apple. Dopamine Warrior never sees your card details, your Apple ID, or your name.

To determine whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a third-party subscription service. RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase receipt and an anonymous identifier that RevenueCat itself generates. There is no account, no login, and no email address in the app, so there is nothing for that identifier to be linked to. This is the only data that ever leaves your device. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

3. What else is stored on your device

  • Your pebble count
  • Your chosen unlock length (5, 10, or 15 minutes)
  • Whether you have completed onboarding

That is the complete list. It stays on your device.

4. No analytics, no ads, no tracking

Dopamine Warrior contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter, and no accounts. No data is used to track you across apps or websites, and none is ever sold or shared with data brokers.

Local notifications — the one the shield posts when you tap "Breathe with Ollie" — are generated and delivered on your device and are never sent through a server.

5. Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything, because everything lives on your device. If you have made a purchase and want the anonymous RevenueCat record removed as well, email the address below and it will be deleted.

6. Children

Dopamine Warrior is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page changes with it, and the previous versions remain visible in this site's public revision history.
