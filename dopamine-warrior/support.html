Common questions

Ollie's shield shows the standard iOS block screen, not Ollie.
Restart your phone once. If it persists, email us with your iOS version.

An app I shielded opened anyway.
Dopamine Warrior needs Screen Time permission to work. Check Settings → Screen Time and make sure Dopamine Warrior still has access; iOS revokes it if Screen Time itself is turned off.

Can Dopamine Warrior see which apps I chose?
No. iOS gives the app anonymous tokens, not app names. It only knows how many apps you picked. See the privacy policy.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: on your iPhone, Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Dopamine Warrior → Cancel. Cancelling keeps your access until the end of the paid period.

I paid but the app still shows the free tier.
Open the paywall and tap Restore. If that does not help, email us.

Will I lose my pebbles if I delete the app?
Yes. Everything is stored on your device only, so deleting the app removes it.
