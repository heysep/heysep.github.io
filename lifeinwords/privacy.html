Privacy Policy

Life in Words · September 7, 2026

Life in Words has no accounts and no sign-in. Your photos, your dex and your trip history live on this iPhone. Exactly one thing ever leaves the device: a single photo that you choose to send by tapping “Save as a discovery” when a word is not in the dex.

What stays on the device

  • Photos. When you press the shutter, the photo is stored in the app’s private folder and becomes your dex card. It is not written to your photo library (only “Save as wallpaper” adds that one rendered image to Photos).
  • Text recognition. Japanese text in your photo is read on the device. Nothing is transmitted at this step.
  • Location. If allowed, the coordinates at the moment of the shutter are stored on the device to tell which country a photo was taken in (that is how trips are split) and to show “met here” on the back of a card. Location is never sent to a server, and every feature works if you decline.
  • Dex and trip records. Which entries you opened and when, and when trips began and ended. Deleting the app deletes them.

What goes to a server — only when you tap “Save as a discovery”

If the dex does not recognise a word and you choose to keep it as a discovery, the app sends one downsized photo and the text read on the device to our server. The server forwards it to an OpenAI model, which returns a word card (headword, reading, meaning, example).

  • The request carries no name, email, phone number or location. It carries only a random identifier created on first launch, used solely to count the daily limit of five.
  • The server logs the recognised text, the unmatched words and the generated card to decide what to add to the dex next. The photo itself is not retained after the card is made.
  • Data passed to OpenAI is governed by OpenAI’s API data policy; we use the API path that is not used for model training.
  • This feature needs a connection and sends nothing unless you tap it.

Camera

Used only while the capture screen is open. Only the photo you take is stored; preview frames are never stored or transmitted.

Home screen widget

The widget shows one recently opened card. That image lives only in the app group container on this device.

Ads and analytics

None. No ad SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash reporter.

Children

Life in Words is a word dex for travellers. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Everything on the device disappears when you delete the app. Server logs contain nothing that identifies you, so we cannot look a person up, but if you write to the address below we will delete records by device identifier.
