Life in Words — words you pick up on a trip to Japan become a dex made of your own photos.

I took a photo but no card opened

The dex is a closed list of 400 entries. A card opens only when the text in the photo is on that list and legible. Move closer so the sign or menu text fills about half the frame, shoot it straight on, and try again. If it still does not open, use “I don’t know → Save as a discovery”.

Why only five discoveries a day?

Discovery cards are generated on a server, so they are limited to five per day. The count resets at midnight UTC. Opening dex cards has no limit and works offline.

No connection on the subway

Capturing and the dex run entirely on the device, so they work offline. Only “Save as a discovery” needs a connection; the photo is kept so you can retry later.

The widget is empty

Add the widget after you have opened at least one card. It shows the card you opened most recently and refreshes when you open the app.

My trip ended but there was no recap

The recap appears once, the first time you open the app seven days after your last photo in Japan. If you declined location access, use “End trip” on the trip screen.

Deleting photos

This version has no per-card delete. Deleting the app removes every photo, card and record (no photos are kept on the server).
