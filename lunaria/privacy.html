Privacy Policy

Lunaria: Moon Phase & Golden Hour Widget · Last updated September 2, 2026

Everything is computed on your phone. There is no account, no login and no server of ours. Lunaria calculates the moon phase, sunrise, sunset and golden hour locally and draws them on your Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

What we do not collect

  • Name, email, phone number, date of birth or any other identity data
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, call or message history
  • Usage analytics or crash reporters (none are included)

Location

Sun times depend on where you are. If you allow it, Lunaria asks iOS for your approximate location (reduced accuracy, about 1–20 km) and stores the latitude and longitude on the phone only. It is never uploaded. You can decline: moon phases work without location, and Settings → Forget location deletes the stored fix.

What stays on your device

DataWhere
Chosen style, time format, whether you removed ads, approximate locationOne JSON file in the app's private storage
Pre-rendered widget images and a per-day table of phase and sun timesThe App Group container shared with the widget

Deleting the app deletes all of the above.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, the AdMob SDK sends Google device information (model, OS version, language), the app name, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data. If you allow tracking when iOS asks, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is also shared; if you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Lunaria itself receives none of this data.

Purchases

Removing ads is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Purchases can be restored from Settings inside the app.

Children

Lunaria is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
