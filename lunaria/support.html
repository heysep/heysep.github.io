Lunaria Support

Tonight's moon, sunset and golden hour on your Home Screen, in three print styles.

Adding the widget

  1. Open Lunaria once. If you want sun times, tap Use my location.
  2. Long-press your Home Screen, tap the + button, search for Lunaria.
  3. Pick the small or medium size. For the Lock Screen, long-press the Lock Screen → Customize → add a Lunaria widget.

Changing the style

Styles tab → tap Use on Almanac, Swiss or Woodblock. The widget re-draws itself right away.

The widget says "Open Lunaria to refresh"

The widget carries two weeks of frames. Opening the app tops them up.

Sunset time looks wrong

Times are for your approximate location and your phone's time zone. Settings → Update location after travelling. The northern-hemisphere convention is used for which side of the moon is lit.

Purchases

Bought "Remove ads" on another device or reinstalled? Settings → Restore purchases.
