Nibbit

A quiet focus timer. Pay once. iPhone.

Pick a length. Tap start. That is the entire setup — one screen, no wizard, no next-next-next. A small pixel friend sits with you and grows on the minutes you actually put in.

What it does not do

  • No ads. Not a banner, not a video, not one at the end of your session.
  • No subscription. Unlocking every friend is a single purchase, once.
  • No account. Nothing to sign into, so nothing can be lost by being logged out.
  • No streaks. Skipping a day costs you nothing.

Built for a locked screen

Nibbit remembers when your session ends, not a ticking number. Lock the phone, put it in a drawer, switch apps, even force-quit — come back and the time is still right. You are not required to stare at a screen to prove you were away from it.

Works with no internet at all

The app has no networking code. Your minutes never leave your iPhone, because there is nowhere for them to go. No analytics, no crash reporting, nothing.
