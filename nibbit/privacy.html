Privacy Policy

Nibbit · last updated 7 September 2026

Nibbit collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. The app contains no networking code of its own and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Everything you do in Nibbit stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your focused minutes and which friend you are raising.
  • A running session — its start and end time, so the timer survives a restart.
  • Your settings — theme, and whether the end-of-session alert is on.

All of it is kept in the app's own storage on the device. Deleting Nibbit deletes all of it. There is no backup on our side, because there is no our side.

Notifications

If you allow it, Nibbit schedules exactly one local notification per session: the one that rings when your session ends. It is created and delivered by your iPhone. Nibbit sends no push notifications, no reminders and no streak nudges — those do not exist in the app.

Purchases

Unlocking all friends is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Nibbit never sees your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction. We receive no personal information from it.

Children

Nibbit collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated date at the top changes with it.
