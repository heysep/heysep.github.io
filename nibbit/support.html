The timer kept running while my phone was locked. Is that right?

Yes. Nibbit records when your session ends, not a ticking counter, so locking the screen, switching apps or even force-quitting does not disturb it. You do not have to keep the screen on.

I stopped early. Did I lose my minutes?

No. Nibbit credits the minutes you actually focused. There is no penalty for stopping, and nothing dies.

I bought the unlock and it is not showing.

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Will there be a subscription?

No. Unlocking all friends is a single purchase. That is the point of the app.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone only. See the privacy policy.
