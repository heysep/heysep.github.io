Privacy Policy

That Moment: Poker Trainer · Last updated 2026-10-02 · Developer: Yosep Lim (heysep)

That Moment collects no personal information. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of ours behind the app. Your progress stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

Progress
The date you started using the app, the moments you finished with your choices and Match results, and the moment you are in the middle of. A backup copy of the previous save is kept next to it, and a copy of a damaged file if one is ever found.Stays on your device.
Settings
Whether reminders are on and at what time, whether the reminder card was shown, when the app last asked for a rating, and whether the last session ended normally.Stays on your device.
PRO status
The last confirmed PRO purchase: the product and when it was checked. It is used to show your status and to work offline.Stays on your device.

The app does not send any of this to us or to anyone else. Because these files sit in the app’s storage, your own iPhone or iCloud backup may include them, as it does for most apps. Backups are between you and Apple.

Purchases

PRO is bought through Apple’s in-app purchase system (StoreKit). Apple handles payment. We never see your card, your Apple Account details or your billing address. The app asks StoreKit whether PRO has been purchased and keeps the cached status described above.

Notifications

Reminders are optional and local. iOS asks for permission when you turn reminders on, either with “Turn on” on the reminder card that appears after a result or in the app’s Settings, and never before. The app then schedules reminders on your device for the next 7 days at the time you choose, at most one a day. The text is fixed and never contains a hand, a card or a player. There is no push server and no push token. You can turn reminders off in the app’s Settings or in iOS Settings.

Ratings

The app may ask for a rating using Apple’s standard prompt. Apple controls that prompt. The app does not receive your rating.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app makes a 9:16 image on your device with the moment number, your Match and the result markers. It does not include cards, player names or links to the original hand. The share sheet may add a link to the app’s App Store page. That link is the same for every user and contains nothing about you. The image is not saved by the app and not uploaded by us. It goes where you send it through the iOS share sheet.

Source links

Each moment lists its sources by site name. Tapping one opens the page in your browser. That site’s own policy applies. The app sends it nothing.

What Apple shares with us

Through App Store Connect, Apple gives developers aggregated numbers about downloads and purchases. If you allow Apple to share analytics with app developers (in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security), the reports can also include usage and crash statistics. These reports do not identify you, and we do not receive your name, email address or Apple Account.

Not a gambling app

That Moment is a study tool. It is not a casino or gambling app. There is no real-money play, no chips or balances, no in-app currency, no prizes and no cash-outs. Nothing you do in the app can win or lose money.

Deleting your data

In the app, Settings, Reset progress erases your finished moments and the moment in progress, and removes the backup copies. It keeps your reminder settings and your purchase status. Deleting the app removes everything the app stored on your device. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

PRO is a one-time purchase, so there is no subscription to cancel. Refunds are requested from Apple.

Third parties

The app has no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code. It uses Apple’s system services: StoreKit for purchases, local notifications, the share sheet and the rating prompt.

AI

The app does not run any AI and does not send your data to any AI service. The short reading notes were drafted with AI before release, reviewed by the editor, and ship inside the app.

Children

That Moment is made for adults and is not directed to children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date changes with it.
