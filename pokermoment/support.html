PRO purchase

Is PRO a subscription?

No. PRO is a one-time purchase, so nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel. To ask for a refund, use Apple’s “Report a Problem” page.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the PRO screen or the app’s Settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles refunds, not the app. Sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com, tap “I’d like to”, choose “Request a refund”, then pick That Moment. Apple decides on the request.

Your progress

My progress is gone.

Progress is saved on your device. It disappears if you delete the app, or move to a new phone without a backup. If you use iPhone or iCloud backup, restoring the backup brings it back. Settings, Reset progress erases it on purpose and cannot be undone.

Why can’t I play a moment again?

Once you have seen how a moment played out, a second try would tell you nothing about your play, so a finished moment stays closed. Its result stays open. To start over on everything, use Settings, Reset progress.

Why is the next moment locked?

The free version lets you start one moment a day, and the next one opens tomorrow. PRO opens the rest of the library.

Reminders

Reminders do not arrive.

Check three things. In iOS Settings, Notifications, That Moment is allowed. In the app’s Settings, the reminder is on. And there is a moment left to play: no reminder is scheduled for a day you already finished, or once nothing is left.

The app schedules the next 7 days each time you open it, so open the app after a long break.

Moments

What do Cross-checked, Reconstructed and Recorded mean?

Cross-checked: every value the game uses agrees in at least two sources. Reconstructed: at least one value is missing, single-sourced or in conflict. Recorded: copied whole from one complete machine-made hand history.

A value the sources cannot settle is shown as Not recorded.

What does Match measure?

The share of your decisions that matched what the player did. The action has to be the same, and for bets and raises the size has to be the same step or one step away. It compares, it does not judge. A match is not a sign of good play.

I found a mistake in a moment.

Email me the moment number (for example #012) and what looks off. Corrections arrive in app updates. A correction does not change a result you already have, and the result screen tells you if a moment was fixed after you played it.

Is this a gambling app?

No. It is a study tool. There is no real money, no chips or balances, and nothing you do can win or lose money.
