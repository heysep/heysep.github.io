The whole guide — every species, every illustration, and the occurrence map — ships inside the app. Airplane mode on a gravel road works exactly like full bars.

What's in it

  • 329 North American game fish: bass, trout, walleye, pike, catfish, crappie, drum, gar, bowfin, salmon, and the saltwater fish you meet from a pier
  • Original side-on illustrations so you can match markings
  • Occurrence data at roughly 11 km resolution
  • A logbook — photo, date, place, length, all optional
  • An aquarium where the species you've landed swim

Your catches stay yours

No account, no sign-up, no cloud. Photos and coordinates are written to your phone and go nowhere else. Bought once — no subscription, no ads, nothing locked behind a "pro" tier.

Notes

Occurrence records come from GBIF, filtered to openly licensed data (CC0 and CC BY); attribution is listed in the app. Records show where a species has been observed, not a guarantee it is biting — follow your state's regulations, seasons, and limits.
