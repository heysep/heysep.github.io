Privacy Policy

Reelbook · Last updated September 7, 2026

Reelbook does not collect your data. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Everything the app knows about you stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

DataWhere it livesLeaves your device?
Catch records (species, date, length, notes) A database inside the app's own containerNo
Photos you attach to a catch Files inside the app's own containerNo
Coordinates saved with a catch Alongside that catch recordNo
Your current location Held in memory only, to look up nearby speciesNo

Permissions

All three are optional. Declining any of them leaves the rest of the app fully usable.

PermissionUsed for
Location, while using the app Looking up which fish species have been recorded near you, and attaching a place to a catch you log. The coordinate is never transmitted.
CameraPhotographing a catch.
Photo libraryAttaching a photo you already have.

No third parties

Reelbook contains no advertising, no analytics SDK, no crash reporting, and no social login. It makes no network requests while you use it — the species guide and the occurrence map are bundled inside the app.

Children

The app is safe for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete a catch inside the app to remove its record and photo. Deleting the app removes everything at once. Because nothing was ever uploaded, there is nothing to request from us.

Data sources

Species occurrence records are derived from the Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF), filtered to records published under CC0 1.0 and CC BY 4.0. Attribution for CC BY datasets is listed inside the app under Settings → Data sources. Fish illustrations are original work created for this app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
