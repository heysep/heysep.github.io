Reelbook Support

A field guide and logbook for what you catch.

The Nearby tab is empty

Two reasons this happens. First, location permission — the tab needs to know where you are, and the app never asks twice. Turn it back on in Settings → Reelbook → Location.

Second, coverage. The species data covers United States waters. Outside the US the Nearby tab will stay empty, but the Dex and Tank tabs work anywhere.

A fish I caught isn't in the guide

The guide holds 329 species chosen because they are actually caught by anglers — small baitfish and darters were left out on purpose. If a species you fish for is missing, email me the name and where you caught it and I'll add it in an update.

Does it work without signal?

Yes, entirely. The guide, the illustrations, and the occurrence map are all inside the app. It makes no network requests at all.

Where are my photos stored?

On your phone, in the app's own storage. They are never uploaded. Deleting a catch removes its photo; deleting the app removes everything.

Is there a subscription?

No. You bought the app once and that's all of it. No pro tier, no ads, nothing locked.

Where does the species data come from?

Occurrence records come from GBIF, filtered to openly licensed datasets (CC0 and CC BY). Records show where a species has been observed — they are not a prediction that it is biting. Always follow your state's regulations, seasons, and limits.
