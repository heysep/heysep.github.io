Privacy Policy

Reset Radar · October 3, 2026

Summary

Reset Radar has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. The server keeps only what it needs to send you a notification. You can delete it from Settings.

What the server stores

ItemWhy
Push token (APNs) and a random device IDTo send a notification to your phone
Language settingTo write the notification in your language
Your alert rules (minimum level, quiet hours, provider incidents on/off)To decide which detections to send you
Delivery record (event ID, device ID, time)To avoid sending the same alert twice

It is not linked to your name, email, contacts or location. None of these are collected.

What is stored on your phone

A cache of recent events, your settings, and whether you own Pro. Purchases are handled by Apple (StoreKit). We do not receive your payment details.

Public posts

The detector reads public posts and pages. For each signal it keeps the source name, a link, the post time and a one-line reason. It does not republish the full text of posts.

Retention and deletion

Detected events are kept for up to 365 days. Your device record and alert rules stay until you choose Settings > Delete my data. The app removes local data only after the server confirms deletion. If deletion fails, the app shows a retry button. You can also email us and we will delete it.

Tracking and third parties

No advertising identifier, no analytics SDK, no tracking. Notifications are delivered through Apple Push Notification service. Purchases go through the App Store.

Children

The app is not directed to children.
