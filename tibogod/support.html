I do not get push notifications

Open iPhone Settings > Notifications > Reset Radar and allow notifications. Alerts are sent only when a reset is detected, so silence can be normal. Check the Status tab to see which sources are live.

An alert was wrong or I missed a reset

Detection uses public signals and is not perfect. Email the time and what you saw. A wrong alert helps tune the rules.

Restore Pro

Open Settings > Pro > Restore Purchases with the Apple ID you bought with.

Delete my data

Settings > Delete my data. Or email and we will delete the device record by the push token or device ID shown in the app.
