Privacy Policy

Upright: Heavy Sleeper Alarm · Effective September 3, 2026

Your alarms stay on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours. Upright is an alarm app for heavy sleepers: it rings even on Silent or Focus, and it keeps ringing until you finish a wake-up mission.

What we do not collect

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any other identity information
  • Location, contacts, calls, or messages
  • Usage analytics or crash reports (we did not add any)

What stays only on your device

DataWhere
Alarms (time, repeat days, label, sound, mission type), mission settings, and your Pro purchase statusThe app's private storage on your iPhone

Deleting the app deletes this data from your device. We hold no copy and cannot restore it.

Permissions

Alarms (AlarmKit). Upright asks iOS for permission to schedule alarms. iOS uses this to ring your alarms, including when your phone is on Silent or in a Focus mode. Nothing about the permission or your alarms leaves the device.

Camera. The camera is used only for the QR / barcode mission, when you choose to stop an alarm by scanning a code. The camera image is processed on the device to read the code and is never saved, uploaded, or shared. If you do not use that mission, the app never asks for the camera.

Motion. The shake and walk missions read the phone's motion sensors to count shakes or steps. The readings are used in the moment to complete the mission and are not stored or transmitted.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob. To deliver ads, the AdMob SDK sends Google information such as device model, OS version, language, app name, an approximate location derived from IP address, and how you interact with ads.

On first launch the app asks once for iOS App Tracking Transparency permission. If you allow tracking, your advertising identifier (IDFA) may be used for personalized ads. If you do not, you will see non-personalized ads instead. Every feature of the app works the same either way. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

To opt out of personalized advertising, turn off Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → Personalized Ads, and use Google's controls at adssettings.google.com. How Google uses advertising data: policies.google.com/technologies/ads · Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Purchasing Pro removes the banner ad.

In-app purchase

Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details, or Apple ID. The app only learns whether the purchase was made so it can unlock Pro and hide ads. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Upright is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. Any change that adds data collection will be announced in the app update notes.
