Upright: Heavy Sleeper Alarm

Upright is an alarm app for people who sleep through everything. If something is not working or you want a feature, email us. We usually reply within a few days.

limyosep0274@gmail.com

What the app does

  • Alarms that actually ring. Upright uses Apple's AlarmKit, so alarms ring at full volume even when your iPhone is on Silent or in a Focus mode.
  • Missions. To stop an alarm you complete a mission — shake the phone, walk a number of steps, solve a math problem, or scan a QR / barcode you placed somewhere away from the bed.
  • Backup rings. If you dismiss the alarm without finishing the mission, Upright keeps ringing again for up to 5 minutes until the mission is done.
  • Pro. A one-time purchase that removes the banner ad. No subscription. Use Restore Purchases in Settings after reinstalling or switching to a new iPhone signed in with the same Apple ID.

Frequently asked

My alarm did not ring.
Open the Settings app → Upright → Alarms and make sure alarms are allowed. Upright requires iOS 26 or later; on earlier versions AlarmKit is not available and the app cannot ring. Also check that the alarm is switched on in the app and that the phone is not powered off — alarms cannot ring on a powered-off iPhone.

How do I stop the backup rings?
Open Upright and finish the mission for that alarm. The backup rings stop as soon as the mission is completed, or on their own after 5 minutes.

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up and no server. Your alarms are stored only on your iPhone.

I bought Pro but still see ads.
Go to Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID that made the purchase.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds go through Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase. We cannot issue refunds ourselves.
